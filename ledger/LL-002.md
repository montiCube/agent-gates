# LL-002. The newest accepted configuration event decides. No clock does.

Anonymised from the source system's ledger.

**Date.** 2026-09-24.

**Decision.** The ladder in LL-001 is deleted. A dormant helper that also cut weight by age, with no production caller, is deleted too, so that no later caller can bring the clock back.

The service acts on the newest accepted configuration event. An event is refused if any of these is true:

- It is 36 hours old or more.
- Its version is not the one the service runs.
- It is not newer than the event already in force.
- It is empty and carries no declaration saying it is meant to be.

A failed or absent read holds new actions. Weight comes from the evidence recorded with the configuration, with a floor of 0.1. Age plays no part.

**Reasoning.** Under LL-001, elapsed time decided how much the service acted. The human moved that decision to an explicit event, which is either accepted or refused. The change followed 12 numbered rulings by the human.

**Supersedes.** LL-001, in whole.

**Commit.** In the private source repository. Not reproduced.
