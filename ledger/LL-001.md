# LL-001. Late configuration falls back through a ladder

Superseded by LL-002 on 2026-09-24.

Anonymised from the source system's ledger.

**Date.** On or before 2026-06-03, the day the source ledger was reconciled. The original entry date was not extracted.

**Decision.** Each cycle, the service looks for its configuration in this order:

1. A fresh copy.
2. A stale copy, acted on at reduced weight.
3. A sidecar copy saved from the last good read.
4. None. The service holds.

The weight of a stale copy is `max(0.25, 1 - hours_stale / 48)`. It reaches the 0.25 floor at 36 hours.

**Reasoning.** Keep acting when configuration arrives late, rather than stopping, and act less as it ages.
